Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Zero2Gig is a learning website that teaches digital skills. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your Google account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We never see your Google password.
  • Your learning progress. We save your answers, points, streaks, finished classes and project review results so you can continue on any device.
  • Your project submissions. When you send a project for review, the link, file and explanation you submit are sent to our AI reviewer to produce feedback. We save the review result (score and feedback). We do not keep the files you upload.
  • Settings on your device. Your browser stores a copy of your progress and your light or dark mode choice.

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To save and show your progress and points.
  • To review your projects and give you feedback.
  • To understand how many people use Zero2Gig and improve the classes.

We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.

Services we rely on

We use trusted companies to run the site. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Google for sign-in.
  • Supabase to store accounts and progress.
  • Vercel to host the website.
  • Groq to run the AI that reviews project submissions.

Links to other sites

The resource library links to files and websites we do not control, such as Google Drive and MEGA. Their own privacy policies apply when you open them.

Your choices

  • You can ask us for a copy of your information or ask us to delete your account and progress by emailing us.
  • You can remove Zero2Gig’s access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings.
  • Please do not put passwords, bank details or other private information in a project submission.

Children

Zero2Gig is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
